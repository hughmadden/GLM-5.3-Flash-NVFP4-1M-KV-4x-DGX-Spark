## 2. Patch 0012 — the idle-time staleness flusher (commits 3c11d98d,
71a81779, 9a415e40 — two live crash fixes baked)

Hugh's policy: stale content (>1h default) flushed in idle time, decode
tax zero by construction. Implementation: per-step scan on **decode-free
steps** (num_scheduled_tokens ≤ 2 gate), population from the block
pool's hash index, ages from an in-connector last-seen map, in-place

synthetic `idle-flush` jobs through the normal manager admission.
Live crash fixes during bring-up: BlockHashToBlockMap has no `.items()`
(scan) and its `_cache` values can be bare blocks or id-dicts (both
handled); the applier's false-positive "already applied" heuristic
removed after it masked a missing hunk twice.

Measured: the flusher writes at the background pump rate (≈2–4 MB/s;
4.62 GB landed in a 36-min tuned run, census-verified), the engine stays
healthy, and the restore path READS the flushed bytes back (1.12 GB of
renewed touched rows during a restore attempt).
